Who we are

A founder who lived it, and advisors who've been here from the start.

Stephen Burke
Founder and CEO

Stephen started his first business young and has worked on people problems ever since, as a systems engineer, a yoga teacher and a shared-housing manager in Sacramento. Campus Mind is where those threads meet.

"I started Campus Mind because of my own move from home to college. Asking for help didn't feel familiar, so I waited until things reached a breaking point before looking for answers. If I'd had a way to see myself as a whole person, check in privately on what felt heavy and find support that fit, I would have acted long before then."
