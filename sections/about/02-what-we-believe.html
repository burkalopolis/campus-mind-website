What we believe

Designed around the student.

  • Students own their story.

    Their wellbeing record belongs to them, not to the campus and not to us.

  • Students start the conversation.

    No one is flagged. A student chooses when, and with whom, to talk.

  • Campuses see patterns, not people.

    Only anonymous totals reach the institution, small groups stay hidden, and nothing flows back to an individual.

  • Faculty time stays protected.

    Early check-ins help students find support sooner, so less lands on you late in the term.
