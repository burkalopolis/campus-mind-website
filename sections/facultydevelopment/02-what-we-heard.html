What we heard

Faculty already do this work. The structure around them doesn't hold.

Interviews with faculty and administrators across California, through NSF I-Corps, kept surfacing the same four patterns.

  • Students go to faculty first.

    "Students trust faculty. They don't trust the financial aid officer."

    Nursing professor, Sacramento State

  • Good programs don't last.

    A faculty wellness program reached faculty who each reached hundreds of students. It ended when its lead's position was eliminated.

  • The cost lands on the helper.

    "Faculty members have different needs, not just the students... we need to know how to support them and also supporting us."

    Education professor, Sacramento State

  • Data stops at the flag.

    "Oh, did it work? Did it even work?"

    Sierra College administrator, on how faculty feel when a referral goes quiet
