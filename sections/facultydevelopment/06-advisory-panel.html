Campus Advisory Panel

Help shape it with us.

The Campus Advisory Panel (CAP) brings together faculty, staff and administrators from community colleges and universities to shape Campus Mind while it's being built.

  • What you'd do

    Try new tools in your class first, give feedback on drafts, and join a short call each term. About 1 hour a month.

  • What you get

    Early access, a direct line to the team, and credit for what you help build.

  • Who we need

    Instructors who already talk with students about life outside class, department chairs, and teaching center or student affairs leads.

Use the form below to join. Requests are answered in the order received, within 3 business days.
