Why early

Reach students before small worries grow.

Most students wait to ask for help until something has already gone wrong. An early, light check-in lets them name what's going on while it's still small.

  • In the classroom

    The syllabus statement and a week-1 class check-in show you're open before the first late assignment.

  • At transitions

    First semester, a first year in campus housing, or a move far from home: the times students most need to know where to start.

"Make sure you have support systems in place to help you be successful in this program." A Sacramento State nursing faculty leader, to students before day one
