Ways to start

Start where you are. Grow when you're ready.

Open to every instructor, from lecturers and adjuncts to tenured faculty, in any discipline, on your own or with your department. Stopping at the first step still counts.

  • Available now

    Add the syllabus statement

    About 10 minutes, once

    Add one paragraph to your syllabus and share the student handout. Students learn you're open to talking and where to start.

  • Live in the pilot

    Run a class check-in

    About 5 minutes, start and end of term

    Students scan one QR code and check in privately. Your class sees anonymous totals, so everyone sees they're not alone.

  • Coming next

    Check in every week

    A few minutes each week

    A short, private weekly check-in so students can find support early and see how things change across the term.
