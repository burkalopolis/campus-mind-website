Who sees what

Students own their information.

  • The student

    Everything: what feels heavy, any closer look and the resources they open. It's theirs to keep, and theirs to share.

  • You, the faculty

    No individual results, ever. If your class checks in together, you see anonymous class totals once at least 5 students take part.

  • The campus

    Anonymous totals only, such as how many students picked Financial this term. Never names.

Designed so students stay in control. No one is flagged.
