About Campus Mind

Campus Mind gives faculty a simple way to point students to a private check-in, gives students a space they own to understand their whole wellbeing, and gives campuses the anonymous insight they need to keep support going. The company grew out of the National Science Foundation's I-Corps program and is based in Sacramento, California.

  • Founder and CEO Stephen Burke
  • Based in Sacramento, California
  • Built for Faculty at CSU and UC campuses, and their students
  • Programs NSF I-Corps (UC Davis Regional Hub); USI CDFI Bootcamp & Pitch Challenge
