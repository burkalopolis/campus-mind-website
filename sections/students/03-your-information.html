Privacy

Your information is yours.

  • No names needed

    The check-in doesn't ask for your name, email or student ID.

  • Your professor never sees your answers

    With a class code, your professor sees only class totals, and only once at least 5 students have checked in.

  • Only totals, ever

    Answers are used only as anonymous totals to learn what students find heavy. No one is ever flagged.
